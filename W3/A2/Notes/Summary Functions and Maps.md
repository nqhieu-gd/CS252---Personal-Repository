* data.describe() gives summary of the data.
* data.mean() give average number of the data (column).
* data.unique() give a list of unique data only.
* data.value\_counts() gives a list of unique data and their counts.
* data.map(lambda p: F(p)) with F(p) is a function of p. This will map the chosen column data.
* data.apply(F(p), axis=type), type is either columns or index.
* data.head() show the first 5 rows of that data.
* data.d\_name\_1 + symbol + data.d\_name\_2 format a column exactly like it.
