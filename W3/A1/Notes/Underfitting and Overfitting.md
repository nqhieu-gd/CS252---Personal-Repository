* Recall the first lesson, the decision tree may have a lot of splits between the top level and a leaf. k split yield 2^k leaves.
* When there a more split, there a more leaves, thus there are less houses in a leaf. The less house in a leaf, the closer the result is to those actual data. But new data may yield unreliable prediction for new data since the dataset is fewer.
* This is called **overfitting**.
* Overfitting yields unreliable prediction. But if the tree is shallow, the houses are not correctly distinct.
* This results in the prediction being far off for most houses, in training data or not.
* This is called **underfitting**.
* We want to mix both **overfitting** and **underfitting**.
